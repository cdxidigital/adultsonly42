# FleshSesh Platform — Chargeable Services & Tools

**Status:** Commercial catalog  
**Scope:** Fan products, creator tools, agency services, Chaturbate white-label cams, WhoreDash companion directory  
**Positioning:** Not another OnlyFans clone. A stacked adult operating system: live cams + companion directory + creator tools + academy + digital twins, with a take on every layer.

**Core hypothesis:** Charge **fans for access**, **creators for leverage**, **agencies for infrastructure**, and **third parties for placement**. Chaturbate white-label and WhoreDash sit as two high-traffic engines that feed the rest.

## 1. Live cams — FleshSesh Cams (Chaturbate white-label)

Chaturbate pays affiliates **20% lifetime revshare** on token spend from users who register through the branded domain. Tracking is domain-permanent, not a cookie.

Existing property: `webcams.fleshsesh.world`

### What we sell

| Product | Who pays | Charge | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Token packs (passthrough) | Fan | CB rates (~US$10.99 / 100 tokens) | FleshSesh takes 20% of spend. Do not markup tokens — CB owns billing. |
| Featured room / homepage takeover | Model or studio | A$49–299 / day | Sold on the FleshSesh frontend. Pure platform margin. |
| Niche cam hubs (AU, Perth, couples, kink) | Fan (optional premium filter) | Free + A$9.99/mo “Cams+” | Filters, recents, follow lists, “models online now that match your tags.” |
| Studio dashboard | Agency | A$99–499 / mo | Multi-model roster, shift calendar, tip analytics from CB affiliate stats. |
| Cam-to-clone upsell | Fan | Bundle | After a show: unlock digital twin / PPV vault. Highest LTV move. |

### FleshSesh layer on top of CB

- Custom domain, logo, colours, AU-first categories
- “Signed talent” badge for roster models
- **Do not** run an independent payment rail for tokens. Let CB absorb compliance and chargebacks.
- Extra money is **placement + membership + funnel into WhoreDash / Academy / twins**, not token markup.

### Setup

Chaturbate affiliate panel → add whitelabel → point custom domain + SSL. Approval typically days, not months.

- Report / block / underage-abuse pipeline before scaling ads

WhoreDash should deep-link to **FleshSesh Cams** (“watch her live”) and **Academy** (“screening & safety module”) so directory traffic is not a dead end.
